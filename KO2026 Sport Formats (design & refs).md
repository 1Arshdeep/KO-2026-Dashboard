# Khalsa Olympics 2026 — Sport Formats & Rules (for design + referees)

**Event:** July 25–26, 2026 · Paramount Fine Foods Centre, Mississauga
**Two days:** Saturday July 25 and Sunday July 26.

> Items marked **(confirm)** are sensible defaults not yet finalized by organizers.
> Basketball & volleyball run in the gyms; soccer runs on the outdoor/indoor fields.

---

## ⚽ SOCCER

- **Teams:** 14, split into **2 groups of 7** (Group A and Group B).
- **Days played:** BOTH days.
  - **Day 1 (Sat):** Group stage — all group games, **11:00 AM to 4:28 PM** (fields cleared by ~4:30 for the 5:00 PM gym tournaments).
  - **Day 2 (Sun):** Knockout — Semifinals, 3rd-place, Final (late morning / early afternoon).
- **Game length:** **20 minutes** per group game, with an **8-minute break between slots** (28-minute slot cadence). Groups finish 4:28 PM. Sunday knockout games: 20 min **(confirm — can be longer for finals)**.
- **Back-to-back limit:** no team plays more than **3 games in a row** before a break.
- **Games guaranteed per team:** **6** (you play every other team in your group of 7 — a full round-robin).
- **Fields:** 4 — Field A, B, C, D (group stage). Knockout uses Field A/B.
- **Timeouts:** none — halftime break only. **(confirm)**
- **How it works (bracket):**
  1. Round-robin inside your group: everyone plays all 6 others. Win = 3 pts, Draw = 1, Loss = 0.
  2. Each group ranked by **points → goal difference → goals scored**.
  3. **Top 2 of each group advance** (4 teams total).
  4. Semifinals cross the groups: **Group A #1 vs Group B #2** and **Group B #1 vs Group A #2**.
  5. Semifinal losers play the **3rd-place** match; winners play the **Final**.

## 🏀 BASKETBALL

- **Teams:** 14, in **one standings pool** (no groups).
- **Days played:** BOTH days.
  - **Day 1 (Sat):** Standings round — clean 30-min rounds **5:00 PM to 10:20 PM** (games at 5:00, 5:30, 6:00 … 10:00).
  - **Day 2 (Sun):** **Top-8 knockout** on Gyms 2 & 3 — quarterfinals 3:30 PM, semifinals 4:20, bronze 4:45, **Final 5:40–6:10 (30 min, played alone)**. Interleaved with the volleyball playoff (see note).
- **Game length:** **20 minutes** (running clock), **10-minute break** between games (30-min slot cadence, clean :00/:30 start times).
- **Games guaranteed per team:** **3** (Saturday).
- **Courts:** 2 — Gym 2 and Gym 3.
- **Timeouts:** **1 per team per game (60 seconds)**. **(confirm)**
- **Saturday matchups are SEEDED** by the pre-tournament ranking so the strongest teams are protected (top teams don't play each other and face mid/lower opponents), giving the true top-8 the best chance to advance. **Exception (by request):** Babbar Khalsa Ballers plays two marquee games — vs KLF and vs Sher Elite — plus McMaster University, all after 7 PM.
- **How it works (bracket):**
  1. Each team plays 3 games Saturday.
  2. All 14 teams ranked in one table by **number of wins**; ties broken by **score differential** (points scored − points allowed). No draws in basketball.
  3. A no-show/forfeit = opponent gets a win and **+10 score differential**.
  4. **Top 8 advance** to Sunday: **QF1 #1 vs #8, QF2 #4 vs #5, QF3 #2 vs #7, QF4 #3 vs #6** → semifinals → bronze → final.
  5. Semifinal losers play **3rd place**; winners play the **Final**.
- **Sunday interleave (both sports share the 3:30–6:30 gym window):** basketball **quarterfinals** run alongside the volleyball **semifinals**; the volleyball **bronze** runs alongside the basketball **semifinals**; then basketball bronze (alone), volleyball final (alone), basketball final (alone). **The two finals never overlap.** Everything ends by **6:10 PM**.
- **Note:** Saturday schedule is built so no team playing both basketball and volleyball is ever double-booked — every such team gets at least a **20-minute break** between games. (On Sunday, a team reaching both the basketball top-8 and volleyball top-4 has its quarterfinal/semifinal staggered on the day.)

## 🏐 VOLLEYBALL

- **Teams:** 14, in **one standings pool** (includes Waymakers).
- **Days played:** BOTH days.
  - **Day 1 (Sat):** Standings round in Gym 1 — 5:00 PM to ~10:55 PM. Each team plays **2 games**.
  - **Day 2 (Sun):** Top-4 knockout on **Gym 1 (single court)**, **interleaved with basketball** — semifinals 3:30 & 3:50, bronze 4:15, then the **Final 5:10–5:36 (played alone)**. Semis & bronze are single 20-min games (timeout + halftime inside the 20 min); the **Final is best of 3 sets × 8 min (1-min break between sets)**.
- **Game length:** **15 minutes** per game.
- **Games guaranteed per team:** **2** (Saturday).
- **Court:** 1 — Gym 1.
- **Timeouts:** **1 per team per game (30 seconds)**. **(confirm)**
- **Saturday matchups are SEEDED** so the protected top-4 (Chinguacousy Ekta, Baba Bidhi Chand, Waymakers, Palm Springs) never play each other and face mid/lower opponents — giving them the best chance to advance. Chinguacousy Ekta and Baba Bidhi Chand are never scheduled against each other.
- **How it works:**
  1. Each team plays 2 games Saturday. Rally scoring (every serve is a point); timed 15 min, **most points when time expires wins**.
  2. Teams ranked by **number of wins**, ties broken by **score differential**.
  3. **Sunday playoff (Gym 1, interleaved with basketball):** Top 4 advance → Semifinal 1 (#1 vs #4) 3:30, Semifinal 2 (#2 vs #3) 3:50 (sequential on one court, alongside basketball quarters), bronze 4:15 (alongside basketball semis), then the **Final 5:10–5:36 played alone**. Semis & bronze are single 20-min games; the **Final is best of 3 sets × 8 min (1-min break between sets)**.
- **Total volleyball games:** 14 (Saturday) + 4 (Sunday) = **18 games**.

### For the volleyball referee's question (how many days / first & second day)

- **First day (Saturday):** all **14** volleyball teams play **2 games** each in **Gym 1**, **15 minutes** each, rally scoring, timed. This is the standings round that decides seeding.
- **Second day (Sunday):** a **top-4 knockout** on **Gym 1**, interleaved with basketball. Semifinals run one after another on the single court (3:30 & 3:50, alongside basketball quarterfinals), bronze at 4:15 (alongside basketball semifinals), then the **Final at 5:10–5:36 played by itself** (basketball is idle during it). Semis & bronze are single 20-min games (timeout + halftime inside); the **Final is 3 sets × 8 min with a 1-min break between sets**. The final winners go in well-rested (they last played earlier), so no extra buffer is needed.
- **Total games:** 14 on Saturday + 4 on Sunday = **18 volleyball games**.
